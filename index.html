Born in Harrow.
Built on Pride.

Rayners Lane FC was founded in 1933 in the heart of Harrow, Middlesex. For over nine decades, the club has been a constant in the community — a place where local football is played with genuine passion, and where the yellow and green of The Lane means something real.

Home has always been Tithe Farm Social Club on Rayners Lane, just minutes from the tube station that shares the club's name. The ground is part of the fabric of this area — unpretentious, community-built, and proud.

The Lane has never been a club that shouts the loudest. It has always let its football do the talking — representing Harrow, its people, and a borough that deserves to see its team compete at the highest level it can reach.

"Yellow first. The Lane always. Community built, Harrow proud. Since 1933."
Rayners Lane FC

Under Chairman Pete Singh and First Team Manager Gary Pitt, the club enters the 2026-27 season in the Combined Counties Premier Division North with renewed purpose — and the belief that the best days for Rayners Lane FC are still ahead.
