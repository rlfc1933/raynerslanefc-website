Three Years.
Two Promotions.
One Unfinished Mission.

When Pete Singh took ownership of Rayners Lane FC, he brought more than investment — he brought belief. In just two seasons, The Lane climbed from Step 6 to Step 4, winning the Middlesex Premier Cup for the first time in the club's history along the way.

2023-24 was nothing short of remarkable. Runner-up in the Combined Counties Premier North despite a four-point deduction, then winning the play-off final on penalties to reach the Isthmian League — Step 4.

2024-25 was the reality check. Step 4 exposed where the squad needed to be stronger. The Lane returned to Step 5. That chapter is closed.

"His values and ambitions were perfectly aligned with where this football club wants to go."
Pete Singh — Chairman & Owner, on appointing Gary Pitt

Gary Pitt arrives from Hillingdon Borough with his entire backroom team. Last season was not an excuse. It was a lesson. Step 4 isn't a dream — it's the plan.
