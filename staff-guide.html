i What is the Staff Admin?

Your control panel for the whole website.

The Staff Admin is a private control panel that lets you update the public Rayners Lane FC website yourself — no developer, no code, no laptop needed. Add a player, post news, log the match-day takings — hit Save, and the public site updates on its own in about 30 seconds.

Think of it like the club's social media — but for the actual website.

1 Getting In

  1. On your phone or computer, open raynerslanefc.co.uk/admin.html
  2. Tip: tap the share icon → Add to Home Screen so it sits like an app you can open with one tap.
  3. Enter the 8-digit PIN: 1933 2026 (the club's founding year + this season).
  4. You're in. Tap any coloured tile to open that section. Tap ← Back to return. Tap Lock when you're done.

Keep it private

The PIN is for committee & staff only. Don't share it publicly or post screenshots that show it.

2 The Golden Rule

Every section has a yellow "Save" button. Nothing goes live until you press it.

When you press Save you'll see "Saved to site — live in ~30 seconds". Wait half a minute, refresh the public page, and your change is there. If you ever see a red message instead, the change didn't save — read it and try again.

The routine for any section

  1. Open the tile.
  2. Fill in the boxes (or tap + Add to add a player/article/etc.).
  3. Press the yellow Save button.
  4. Wait for the green tick, then check the public page.

Spotlight: Man of the Match & New Signing

These two are your "wow" tiles. Add a player (headshot optional), hit save, and a glossy card appears near the top of the home page — yellow for MOTM, green for a new signing. Any box you leave blank simply won't show. The newest one is the one that's live; add a new one each week and use Remove to clear out old ones.

Make it an app on your phone

Tap the gold Install button (top of the admin, and on the main site) → it adds a Lane icon to your home screen so you can open it like any app. iPhone: if you don't see the button, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

4 Match Day Analytics

The boardroom numbers — chairman access only.

This is where we track the club like a business: attendance, takings (gate, programmes, merch), which players were available and which staff checked in — with charts that build up over the season.

  1. Tap Match Day Analytics. You'll be asked for the analytics password (default petesinghchairman).
  2. Fill in the match: date, opponent, score, attendance and the takings boxes.
  3. Tick the players who were available and the staff who checked in (these are pulled from your Squad & Committee).
  4. Tap + Add Match to Log, then Save Analytics to Site.
  5. The summary cards and charts update automatically.

To change the password, open Analytics and tap Change Analytics Password.

5 Social Media

Open to all staff. The Social Media tile holds The Lane's full posting playbook for the season — content pillars, a Monday-to-Sunday match-week rhythm, a month-by-month plan and our hashtags. There's also a Post Planner: jot upcoming posts, hit Save, and everyone on staff sees the plan.

Golden rule of socials: shoot everything vertical on your phone, tag everyone, and reply to every comment.

6 Your Club Email on Your Phone

Set up your @raynerslanefc.co.uk address (hosted by one.com) in your phone's Mail app.

You'll need

Your full club email (e.g. you@raynerslanefc.co.uk) and the password you were given. If you don't have these, ask the chairman.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account.
  2. Choose Other → Add Mail Account.
  3. Enter your name, your full club email, your password, a description (e.g. "RLFC"). Tap Next.
  4. Make sure IMAP is selected, then enter the server settings in the table below.
  5. Tap Save. Your club email now arrives in the Mail app.

Android (Gmail app)

  1. Open Gmail → tap your profile circle → Add another account.
  2. Choose Other and type your full club email → Next.
  3. Choose Personal (IMAP) → enter your password.
  4. Enter the incoming & outgoing server settings from the table below.
  5. Finish — your club email syncs into Gmail.

one.com server settings (the important bit)

Email / Usernameyour full address, e.g. you@raynerslanefc.co.uk
Passwordyour email password
Incoming (IMAP) serverimap.one.com
IMAP port / security993 · SSL/TLS
Outgoing (SMTP) serversend.one.com
SMTP port / security465 · SSL/TLS

Use the SAME username (your full email) and password for both incoming and outgoing. If sending fails, double-check the outgoing server is send.one.com on port 465.

Prefer the browser?

You can always read club email at one.com/webmail — log in with your full address and password. (The Club Email tile in the admin links here too.)

7 "I Saved It But Don't See It"

  1. Wait 60 seconds. The site rebuilds itself after each save — give it a moment.
  2. Hard refresh the page. Your phone may be showing an old saved copy. On iPhone, close the tab and reopen; on a computer press Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R.
  3. Check you got the green tick. No green message = it didn't save. Red message = read it (usually try again).
  4. Still stuck? Note exactly what you did and tell the chairman — nothing is ever lost, the site saves a full history.

Remember

You genuinely cannot break the website from here. Worst case, a save can be undone. Be brave, post often, make The Lane look huge. 💛
