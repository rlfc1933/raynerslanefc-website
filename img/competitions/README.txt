OFFICIAL COMPETITION ARTWORK
============================
Drop Association-issued competition marks here, then set `logo` on the matching
entry in data/competitions.json to the file path.

Awaiting artwork:
  fa-cup.png    Emirates FA Cup
  fa-vase.png   Isuzu FA Vase

These marks are ISSUED to competing clubs — FA Challenge Vase rule 3(f): a club
"shall use the image issued by The Association". They are not downloadable, and
a traced or approximated substitute breaches the rule it appears to satisfy.

Until the files are here the Studio renders the competition's proper name as
type. That is a correct, permitted treatment — not a broken state.
