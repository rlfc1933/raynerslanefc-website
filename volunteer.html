Rayners Lane FC is built by its community. If you want to be part of The Lane, there's a place for you here.

Match Day Steward
Help manage the gate, welcome fans and ensure everyone has a great experience at Tithe Farm. No experience necessary — training provided.
Home games · 2pm start
Programme Seller
Sell match day programmes at the gate and around the ground. Get to know every fan that walks through the door at Tithe Farm.
Home games · Flexible hours
Club Photographer
Capture the best moments from matchdays. Photos go on the website, social media and in the matchday programme. Perfect for aspiring photographers.
Home & away games
Social Media
Help run the club's social media accounts on matchdays — live commentary, goal announcements, photos and fan engagement. Bring The Lane to life online.
Match days · Remote possible
Groundswork
Help maintain Tithe Farm — pitch marking, ground setup, cleaning and general maintenance. Be the reason The Lane looks its best every match day.
Weekends · Flexible
Stats & Analysis
Help track match statistics, compile reports and assist Gary Pitt's coaching team with data. Great for anyone with a passion for football analytics.
Match days + remote
