GetInvolved

The Lane is your club too. Help with one small job, on a matchday or from home. No committee, no account.

Offer to help

How it
works

  1. Pick something small. One matchday, one job. You don’t need to join a committee.
  2. Tell us below. No account, no forms beyond this one.
  3. The club approves the role before assigning it, and tells you who to meet, where and when.

Ways to help

  • Help on one matchday

    Welcome people at the gate, point newcomers in the right direction, help set up and pack away.

    When
    One home match of your choice
    Where
    Tithe Farm
    Commitment
    About three hours, from an hour before kick-off
    Coordinated by
    The club official running the matchday
    Checks
    None. You won’t be asked to handle money on your own.
    Offer this

  • Take photographs

    Photograph a home match: the team, the ground, the supporters who agree to be in shot. Approved photos are used on the website and socials, credited to you.

    When
    Any home match
    Where
    Tithe Farm
    Commitment
    The match, plus sending your best shots afterwards
    Coordinated by
    A club official, named when your role is confirmed
    Checks
    Follow the club’s photography guidance. No photos of children without the club’s safeguarding approval.
    Offer this

  • Help with programmes

    Help put together or hand out the matchday programme: checking details, collecting team news, getting copies to supporters.

    When
    The week before a home match, or on the day
    Where
    From home, or at Tithe Farm
    Commitment
    An hour or two
    Coordinated by
    A club official, named when your role is confirmed
    Checks
    None
    Offer this

  • Put up posters

    Take a few club-approved posters to shops, cafés and noticeboards near you, with the owner’s permission.

    When
    Whenever suits you, ahead of a home match
    Where
    Rayners Lane, Harrow, Pinner and nearby
    Commitment
    An hour, once or twice a season
    Coordinated by
    A club official, named when your role is confirmed
    Checks
    None. Only posters the club has approved.
    Offer this

  • Help with merchandise

    Help a club official run the merchandise table on a matchday: setting out stock, answering questions.

    When
    Home matches when merchandise is on sale
    Where
    Tithe Farm
    Commitment
    Before kick-off and half-time
    Coordinated by
    A named club official
    Checks
    Involves money: always alongside a club official, never on your own.
    Offer this

  • Introduce a local business

    Know a business owner who might back The Lane? Tell us who, and the club will make the approach itself.

    When
    Any time
    Where
    Anywhere
    Commitment
    A conversation
    Coordinated by
    The chairman
    Checks
    None. Please ask the business before passing on their details.
    Offer this

  • Help with sponsor thank-yous

    Help make sure sponsors get what they were promised: a photo with the match ball, a programme mention checked, a thank-you sent.

    When
    Matchdays with a sponsor
    Where
    Tithe Farm, or from home
    Commitment
    Half an hour on the day
    Coordinated by
    The club’s administrator
    Checks
    None. No access to club accounts or money.
    Offer this

  • Support a community event

    When the club runs an approved community day, help it run smoothly: welcoming, setting up, directing people.

    When
    Announced event by event
    Where
    Tithe Farm or a local venue
    Commitment
    Half a day
    Coordinated by
    The event’s named organiser
    Checks
    If the event involves children, a DBS check and the club’s safeguarding approval come first.
    Offer this

Safeguarding. Any role that involves children or young people, money, club systems or someone’s welfare needs the club’s approval and the right checks before you start. The club decides who does what; nobody is put in that position without it. Questions: info@raynerslanefc.co.uk or our club policies.
