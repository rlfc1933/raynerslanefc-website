What it is

You get

  • A Lane 100 supporter number, from 1 to 100, in the order people join. Numbers are never reused.
  • Your first name on the founding supporters’ wall, only if you tick the box.
  • Club updates by email, only if you ask for them.
  • First word on ways to help: matchdays, bringing friends, small volunteer jobs.

It is not

  • Ownership, shares or a say in how the company is run.
  • An investment or a payment. Joining is free.
  • A promise of merchandise, free tickets or rewards.
  • Needed to watch a match. Everyone is welcome on the gate.
