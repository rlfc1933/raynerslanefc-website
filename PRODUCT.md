# Rayners Lane FC — Product brief (for design work)

**What it is.** The official site of Rayners Lane FC (est. 1933, Harrow): the
club's memory and its voice, plus the volunteer tools that keep both going.
VISION.md is the constitution; this is the short version design needs.

**Who uses it.**
- Supporters on phones, often at the ground on patchy signal — next match, live
  score, result, news, tickets, Fan Zone.
- Players, families and prospective players — squad, trials, contact.
- Sponsors and partners — investment, the sponsor rail, how they are presented.
- Committee volunteers in Staff HQ (`admin.html`) — updating fixtures, scores,
  news and match-day operations, frequently one-handed on a Saturday.

**What success looks like.** A supporter finds the next game and the latest
result in one glance; a volunteer posts a score in seconds without training;
anything the site states about the club is true.

**Non-negotiables.**
- Never fabricate: no invented stats, quotes, testimonials or imagery.
- Specific beats generic: Rayners Lane, not "a football club".
- Fast on a weak phone signal; no build step; self-hosted fonts.
- Accessible: WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard, reduced motion, the a11y panel.
- Approved interfaces are refined, not redesigned, unless a change is justified
  in writing against a measured problem.
