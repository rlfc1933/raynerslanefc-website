# Smallz's guide — Social Media

*Rayners Lane FC portal. Plain English, written from what the portal actually does.*

## First, the reassurance

You cannot break the website from the portal. It can only change club information
and images — never the site's code or design.

## What you see first

**Social and Marketing**:

- **Next fixture** — who, when, home or away
- **Post Studio** — match graphics
- **Captions** — writing help
- **Approved images**
- **News drafts**
- **Recent downloads**

Underneath, **All Club Tools** — everything else, including the programme if
you're contributing to it.

## Making a match graphic

1. Open **Post Studio**
2. Pick the post type
3. The fixture details fill in — **check home or away is right**, it decides which
   crest goes where
4. Choose **Instagram** or **X** sizing
5. Press **Download PNG**

Club fonts and colours are applied for you. You do not need Canva.

## Getting it onto your phone

**On a phone**, the share button opens your normal share sheet — Save to Photos,
WhatsApp, Instagram, AirDrop. Straight into the app you're posting from.

**On a computer**, Download PNG saves to your Downloads folder.

**If you get an error about HEIC**, that's an iPhone photo format the graphic
builder can't read. Pick a JPG or PNG instead.

## Captions

The AI assistant turns a rough note into a caption. It helps with wording — it
**cannot post anything**, and it must never invent a score, a scorer or a quote.
If it does, tell somebody: that's a bug, not a quirk.

Always read it before it goes out. It doesn't know what happened; you do.

## Writing news

You can start an article and save it as a draft. Drafts are **not public**. When
it's ready, submit it — somebody reviews it before anything goes out. The panel
says so: *"Nothing goes out until you approve it."*

## What reaches the public website

| You do this | Public? |
|---|---|
| Download a graphic | **No** — it's on your device |
| Generate a caption | **No** |
| Save a news draft | **No** |
| Submit an article | **No** — waiting for review |
| An article being approved | **Yes** |

## One thing that will catch you out

**Downloaded graphics are not saved anywhere.** Post Studio builds the image,
hands it to your device, and forgets it. There is no library of past graphics yet.

So if you might want it again — **keep it on your device**. Recreating last week's
means re-entering the fields.

## Correcting a mistake

A graphic isn't published by making it, so a wrong one costs you nothing — just
make another. If something wrong reached the website, tell whoever approved it.

## On a phone

Post Studio is genuinely built for it — including the share sheet. Making and
posting a graphic from the ground is a normal way to work.
