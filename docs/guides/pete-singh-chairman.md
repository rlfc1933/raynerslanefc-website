# Pete's guide — Chairman

## What you see first
**Club overview** · **Staff access** · items needing senior attention · publishing
status · sponsor issues · committee activity · security notices. Then **All Club Tools**.

## Your job in the portal
Oversight and access — not approving every article, programme and graphic. If you
became the bottleneck for ordinary work, the club would stop publishing.

## Inviting somebody
Staff Access → fill in name, username, club title, role → **Create setup link**.

You get a link **once**. Copy it and give it to them privately — a text, in person,
however you'd share anything else private. They follow it and choose their own
password.

**You never see their password. Nobody does.** There is no list anywhere. If they
lose the link, create a replacement — that cancels the old one automatically.

Links work once and expire after 72 hours.

## What only you can do
Create staff accounts · reset access · assign Chairman · grant administrative
capability · system settings.

**You cannot promote your own account.** Deliberate: making somebody more powerful
should take a second person, and that includes you.

## Nigel's cover
Nigel can **disable** a compromised account without you — if your account were the
problem, waiting for you to grant permission would be useless. He cannot create
accounts or promote anyone.

## Money
Matchday takings only, protected separately, every entry recording who logged it.
Bank details, invoices and payroll are **not** in the portal and should not be.

## Correcting a mistake
Everything is recorded and reversible. Staff actions carry a name and a time.
