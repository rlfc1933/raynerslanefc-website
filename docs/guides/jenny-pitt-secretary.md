# Jenny's guide — Secretary

*Rayners Lane FC portal. Plain English, written from what the portal actually does.*

## First, the reassurance

You cannot break the website from the portal. It is only allowed to change the
club's information files and images — never the site's code or design. The worst
outcome is wrong words on a page until you fix them.

## What you see first

**Match Day** is your area, and it opens first:

- **Next fixture** — opponent, date, kick-off, home or away, venue
- **Match readiness** — what still needs doing before Saturday
- **Officials** — referee and assistants
- **FWP status** — whether the official feed has our match yet
- **Corrections** — anything that looks wrong
- **Full-time confirmation** — settling the result afterwards

Underneath is **All Club Tools**. Everything else the club uses is there, and you
are meant to use it — if Russell is away and the programme needs a nudge, you can
open it.

## Your normal week

**Early in the week** — open Fixtures and check the next match: date, kick-off,
home or away, venue, competition. Tuesday matches are 7.45pm; Saturdays are 3pm.

**Mid-week** — add match officials as you receive them.

**Before matchday** — check FWP has the fixture. If it does not, that is worth
chasing before Saturday rather than on the day.

## Matchday

Mostly, you watch. The Match Centre runs itself: countdown, then live score, then
full time, reading from Football Web Pages every minute.

- **If the score looks wrong**, it is coming from FWP. The Live Scoreboard has a
  manual override for genuine emergencies.
- **If the page says "Updates delayed — waiting for the latest from the ground"**,
  FWP has gone quiet. It recovers on its own. Nothing is broken.
- **After full time**, confirm the result.

## The line-up is not yours to enter

The line-up supporters see comes from the **official team sheet on FWP**. Gary's
own selection is private and stays private.

So when a programme says the teams are not confirmed, it is waiting for FWP — not
for you, and not for Gary.

## What reaches the public website

| You do this | Public? |
|---|---|
| Change a fixture date, kick-off, venue | **Yes, within seconds** |
| Add match officials | **Yes** |
| Confirm a result | **Yes** |
| Record matchday takings | **No** — internal only |

Every tool carries a badge: *Changes the public website* or *Internal only*. Read
it before you press Save.

## Correcting a mistake

Change it back and press Save again. There is no version to restore or approval
to wait for — fixtures are live data, and the newest correct value wins.

If two of you edit at once, nothing is lost: the portal merges changes rather than
letting the last save wipe the first.

## Covering for someone

You can open any ordinary club tool. Unfinished work belongs to the club, not the
person who started it. Only staff accounts and system settings are restricted.

## On a phone

Everything works. The menu moves to the bottom, and the tools stack into one
column. Matchday from the touchline is a normal way to use it.

## How you know it worked

A confirmation appears and the value you typed is showing. If you see an error
instead, nothing was saved — try again, then ask.
