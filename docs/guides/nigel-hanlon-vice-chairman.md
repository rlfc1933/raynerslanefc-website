# Nigel's guide — Vice Chairman

## What you see first
**Club overview** · **Chairman cover** · access issues · pending publications ·
committee activity · continuity actions. Then **All Club Tools**.

## Your job
Continuity. When Pete is unavailable the club keeps running — you can reach the
ordinary tools and keep work moving.

## What you can do about accounts
**You can disable an account immediately, on your own.**

That is deliberate and worth understanding. The club wanted two people able to
shut down a compromised login. A permission that has to be *granted* first is
useless in the one situation it exists for — if the account in trouble is the
chairman's, there is nobody left to grant it. So you hold it permanently.

**You cannot create accounts or promote anyone**, including yourself. Those need
Pete, or explicit temporary cover.

Disabling stops the login immediately. It does not delete anything — their history
and record stay intact.

## Everything else
You can open any ordinary club tool: fixtures, programme, news, Post Studio,
sponsors. Unfinished work belongs to the club, not to whoever started it.

## What reaches the public website
Every tool carries a badge — *Changes the public website* or *Internal only*.
Read it before you press Save.
