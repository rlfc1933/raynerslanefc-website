# Gary's guide — Team Manager

## What you see first
**Next match** · **Squad selection (private)** · squad information · manager's
notes · match content · programme contribution. Then **All Club Tools**.

## Your selection is private — properly private
This matters more than anything else in your guide, so here it is plainly.

**Your squad selection cannot reach the website.** It is stored somewhere
different, reached by different code, and the programme reads a different source
entirely. There is no Save button anywhere in the portal that publishes it.

The line-up supporters see comes from the **official team sheet on Football Web
Pages** — the one lodged with the competition. Not your working selection.

So you can pick, change your mind, pick again, and nothing leaks. That is by design,
and it was checked.

## Your normal week
- Update squad and availability
- Make your selection when you're ready
- Add manager's notes when Russell or Darren ask for programme copy

## Matchday
The Match Centre runs itself from FWP. You don't need to do anything for the score.

If the programme says teams aren't confirmed, it's waiting for FWP — not for you.

## What reaches the public website
| You do this | Public? |
|---|---|
| Squad selection | **No — never** |
| Squad and player information | **Yes** |
| Manager's notes for the programme | Only once the programme publishes |

## What you don't have
No treasury, no sponsor contracts, no staff accounts, no system settings. Not an
oversight — you don't need them, so they're not yours to worry about.
