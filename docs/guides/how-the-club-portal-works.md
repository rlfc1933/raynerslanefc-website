# How the Club Portal Works

*For everyone at Rayners Lane FC. No technical knowledge needed.*

---

## The most important thing first

**You cannot break the website from the portal.**

This is not reassurance — it is how the system is built. When you press Save, the
portal is only allowed to write to two places: the club's information files
(`data/`) and uploaded images. It physically cannot touch the website's code, its
pages or its design. The worst that can happen is that a page shows the wrong
words until you fix them.

So please click things. Have a look around. You are not going to bring the site down.

---

## The portal is not the website

| | |
|---|---|
| **The portal** | `raynerslanefc.co.uk/admin.html` — where the club works. Only staff can reach it. |
| **The website** | `raynerslanefc.co.uk` — what supporters see. |

Some things you do in the portal change the website straight away. Some do not.
**Every tool tells you which** — look for the badge:

- **Changes the public website** — press Save and supporters see it within seconds
- **Internal only — not public** — nobody outside the club ever sees this

---

## What the words mean

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Written down, kept inside the club. Nobody outside can see it. |
| **Saved** | Your latest work is stored. On tools badged *Changes the public website*, saved also means live. |
| **Submitted** | You have sent it to somebody to check. Still not public. |
| **Approved** | Somebody has checked it. Ready to go out. |
| **Published** | Supporters can see it now. |
| **Archived** | Kept for the record, no longer shown as current. |

## Football Web Pages (FWP)

FWP is the official service the league uses. It is where scores, line-ups and
results come from.

**We do not type in the score.** The Match Centre reads it from FWP every minute
during a match, and updates itself. If FWP is slow, the page says *"Updates
delayed — waiting for the latest from the ground"* rather than showing something
wrong.

**The line-up is FWP's, not ours.** A manager's own team selection is private and
stays private. The line-up supporters see is the official team sheet lodged with
the competition. That is why a programme sometimes says the teams are not
confirmed yet — it is waiting for FWP, not for you.

## The Match Centre

The live page for each match: countdown before kick-off, score and events during,
final result after. It updates on its own. Nobody has to sit and refresh it.

## The matchday programme

Built automatically from information the club already holds — the squad, the
league table, the committee, the sponsors. It publishes on matchday once **both**
official team sheets are confirmed.

**Home matches only.** If we are away, there is no programme, and that is correct.

Once published, an edition never changes. An archived programme shows the club as
it was that day, not as it is now.

## Latest News

Articles for the website. They can be written, saved as drafts, and reviewed
before anything goes out. **Review Drafted Stories** exists for exactly that —
*"Nothing goes out until you approve it."*

## The AI writing assistant

It helps with wording — a first draft, a headline, a caption. **It cannot publish
anything.** A person always reads it and decides. It works from information the
club already holds, and it must never be allowed to invent a score, a scorer, a
quote or an attendance. If it does, that is a bug — please say so.

## Search visibility (SEO and AEO)

The website is set up so search engines and AI assistants understand who Rayners
Lane are, which league we play in and what happened in a match. This mostly looks
after itself. Write like a person; the machinery underneath handles the rest.

## Post Studio

Makes match graphics in club colours and fonts — pick a post, fill in the fields,
choose Instagram or X sizing, download.

**On a phone** the share button opens your normal share sheet: Save to Photos,
WhatsApp, Instagram. **On a computer** Download PNG puts it in your Downloads
folder. You do not need Canva.

## Photographs

Images can be uploaded and used on the website. There is **not yet** a full media
library with photographer credits, match tagging and search — that is honest, and
it is on the list. Today, images are attached where they are used.

## Sponsors

Sponsor records, logos and where they appear on the website and in the programme.

## Money

The portal holds **matchday takings only** — what came through the gate, and
whether it has been reconciled. That is protected separately and every entry
records who logged it.

**Bank details, invoices, payroll and tax records are not in the portal and never
will be.** They belong in proper accounting software. This is deliberate.

---

## Your account

You get a **one-time setup link** from whoever is managing staff access. You
follow it and choose your own password.

**Nobody else ever knows your password — including the chairman.** There is no
list of passwords anywhere. If you forget it, you get a new setup link; nobody
can look the old one up.

Setup links work **once** and expire after **72 hours**. If yours has expired,
just ask for another.

## Covering for each other

Committee members can reach the ordinary club tools, not only their own area. If
somebody is ill, away, or has left, the work does not get stuck behind their
login. **Unfinished work belongs to the club, not to the person who started it.**

A few things stay restricted to the chairman and vice chairman: creating staff
accounts, resetting access, and system settings. Everything else is shared.

---

## If something goes wrong

Nothing you do here is unrecoverable. Every change is recorded, and every change
can be changed back.

If you are unsure whether something went public, look at the badge on the tool.
If you are still unsure, ask — that is always better than guessing.
