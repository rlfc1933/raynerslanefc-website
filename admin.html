📱 Social Media

Open to all staff. This is The Lane's social playbook for the 2026-27 season — what to post, when, and why. Consistency beats perfection. Post like a club three divisions above us.

The Golden Rules
  • Yellow first, always. Every graphic uses club yellow & green. Same fonts, same badge, every time.
  • Faces & emotion win. Players, fans, celebrations > empty pitches and logos.
  • Reply to everything. Every comment, every tag. Engagement is free reach.
  • Tag everyone. Opponents, the league, players, sponsors, Middlesex FA. Borrow their audience.
  • Video > photo > text. A 9:16 vertical clip will always outperform a still.
Where We Play
📸 Instagram — flagship ✖️ X / Twitter — live & news 🎵 TikTok — reach & youth ▶️ YouTube — highlights 📘 Facebook — local & older fans

Shoot everything vertical (9:16) on a phone. One Reel posts natively to Instagram, TikTok & YouTube Shorts — three platforms, one clip.

Content Pillars (rotate these)

⚙️ Settings

Auto-deploy is active. Every Save commits to GitHub through a secure Netlify function and the site goes live in ~30 seconds. The GitHub token is stored safely on Netlify (Site settings → Environment variables → GITHUB_TOKEN) — never in this page. One-time setup only; nothing to do day-to-day.
