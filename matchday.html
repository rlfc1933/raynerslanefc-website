Getting here

  • Address

    Tithe Farm Social Club, 151 Rayners Lane, Harrow HA2 0XH.

  • By Tube

    Rayners Lane station (Piccadilly and Metropolitan lines) is a 12-minute walk.

  • By bus

    Routes H13 and 398 stop nearby, at the Rayners Lane station stop.

  • By car

    Postcode HA2 0XH. Street parking on Tithe Farm Road and surrounding streets.

  • On the day

    The clubhouse bar is open to supporters on matchdays.

  • Access needs

    Tell us what you need before you come and we’ll give you a straight answer about the ground: info@raynerslanefc.co.uk or the contact page.

Sharing
Tithe Farm

Broadfields United also play their home games at Tithe Farm. When we play them as the away side, the match is still at Tithe Farm, but it is their home game. The fixtures page always says which is which.
